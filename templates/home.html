Common questions

Is my data safe?
Yes. Your file is uploaded over HTTPS, scanned on our server, and deleted automatically within 24 hours. We do not share your data with anyone. Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 aware.
What file format do I need?
Excel (.xlsx) or CSV. It looks for columns named name, tin (or kra_pin), bank_account (or account), phone, and email. Close variations are detected automatically. If you don't have a list, download our template.
Why KES 500?
Enterprise vendor MDM platforms cost KES 5M+ per year and are built for 5,000-person companies. One fraudulent vendor payment averages KES 2.4M. KES 500 is roughly 1/10,000th the cost of a single fraud incident.
How do I pay and get the file?
M-Pesa, through Paystack. Enter your email and phone number on the results page, tap pay, and you'll get the M-Pesa prompt on your phone. Once payment confirms, the cleaned CSV is emailed to you as an attachment and also available for direct download.
What happens after I unlock?
You get the full cleaned CSV with a flags column per vendor — every flagged vendor marked with the specific issue: MISSING_TIN, SAME_BANK_DIFF_TIN, NEAR_DUPLICATE_NAME, and so on. Filter in Excel and start calling vendors to verify.
Do you store my file?
We store it on our server only to generate your cleaned report, and delete it automatically within 24 hours. The cleaned file is also emailed to the address you enter — that copy is yours to keep.
